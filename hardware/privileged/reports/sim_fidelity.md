## Not answerable from logs

- Whether the real yaw reading is clockwise-positive *in the cage frame* is what the yaw sign above measures against the stick; a 1-minute hand check (drone on the floor, turn it clockwise, watch `yaw`) confirms it independently.
- Lateral speed (vgy) and the Tello's velocity estimate itself lag the truth; fitted delays include that lag.
