Training

All training is PPO (actor-critic) in our simulator of the real cage, drone and camera, with randomised dynamics so the policy survives the real drone. Each run continues from the previous milestone.

Learning curves

Passes per 90 s episode at each evaluation (36 fixed episodes), against the steps that run added.

The loop optimiser

A script races the best policy so far against one new idea per round, re-scores both on the same 72 episodes plus 60 slanted starts (score = passes/episode + corner entry), and keeps a challenger only if it wins by more than 0.10.

Rounds 1–8 were scored on one 36-episode evaluation, with corners counted at half weight. From round 9 every candidate is re-scored on the same 72 + 60 episodes and corners count fully, so those scores are higher and steadier.
