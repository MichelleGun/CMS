Duration

        

          
          
        

        

          
          
        

        
Trains until you press Stop, e.g. overnight. The
          latest policy is saved at every evaluation (and the final one on Stop), and the PC is kept
          awake while it trains. Keep the dashboard server window open: closing it ends training.

        

          
          
        

        
Each env copy runs in its own process (one CPU core), so more envs
          collect experience faster without changing what is learned.

    Training environment
    
        
    The simulated cage the policy trains in: a 2×2×2 m net with a blue
          noodle ring hung near the centre, posed like the one in the phone scan (1.29 m up, turned and
          leaning slightly). The drone always starts on the floor, must take off, find the ring and
          fly through it in the arrow's direction. Touching the net bounces it back.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Preview
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
        
    
          
    
        
    
          
        
    
        
    "Randomise ring" shows the per-episode variation (position, facing, size)
          used in training to cover repositioning. "Wind sway" shows how the light noodle, hung from a rope
          tied to its top, swings about that knot during an episode (it is shoved by the drone too, in
          training). "Max swing" is how far it can swing; it is the same setting as in the Training panel.
